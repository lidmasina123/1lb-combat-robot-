# 1lb-combat-robot-
The concept is a 1 lb combat robot with an extremely powerful drive system and a spinning disk mounted on the end of an articulating arm. The goal is to attack opponents from above and target their top armor, which is often the weakest area of a combat robot.

Mounting the weapon on a fast-moving arm helps the disk achieve good bite on impact. This allows the blade to spin at high speed and store a large amount of energy before each hit, making the attacks much more effective.

I have built saw-style robots before, but those designs lacked drive power and were not very destructive. For this robot, I wanted to combine a much stronger drivetrain with a more energetic weapon so it can both control opponents and deliver harder hits.
